Book 11 of Saving Ceraste – is officially up for presale!

Goober has officially entered the chat.

 \*\**

So. You know how for ten books, we've watched our Cerastean males stumble their way through Earth customs, mispronounce things, and generally lose their minds over human women? You know how they learned our language, adopted our weird habits, and tried very hard to understand why we're so obsessed with dogs and movies?

Well, buckle up, because this time we're flipping the script.

 Vel'shar is the first book in the series told from a Cerastean female's perspective. Our FMC is A'Vanti – an architect, a survivor, and one of the most fiercely stubborn women I've ever written. She doesn't do helpless. She doesn't do damsel-in-distress. Even when she was too weak to stand, she was still hissing at armed soldiers and throwing herself in front of people she loved.

 I am absolutely obsessed with her.

 And her love interest? A human fighter pilot named Cody Johnson whose military call sign is Goober. He's sweet, he's funny, he's completely in touch with his feelings, and he's set his sights on A'Vanti.

 This is the first book where we leave Earth behind and go to Ceraste itself. I'm talking ancient architecture, alien landscapes, bizarre predators, twin suns, desert cities — the full experience. For ten books, we've been orbiting Earth. Now we finally get to see a whole new planet.

 I poured my whole heart into building this world, and I really think you're going to love it.
